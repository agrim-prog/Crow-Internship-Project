THIS LEASE AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made and entered into this second day of September, two thousand twenty-six, by and between the Landlord and Coastal Design Studio LLC (hereinafter referred to as "Tenant"), regarding the real property located at 77 Harbor View Terrace, Suite 5 (the "Premises").

The term of this lease shall commence on October 1, 2026, and shall continue for a period of thirty-six full calendar months, expiring on September 30, 2029.

In consideration for the rights of use and occupancy granted under this Agreement, Tenant covenants to pay to the Landlord Annual Base Rent in the amount of Forty-Two Thousand and 00/100 Dollars ($42,000.00), payable in twelve equal monthly installments in advance on or before the first business day of each calendar month.

Tenant shall reimburse Landlord for its proportional share of Common Area Maintenance, fixed at nine percent of total aggregate costs, payable within fifteen days of receipt of an invoice.

No escalation of Annual Base Rent shall occur during the initial Term.

Tenant shall have no option or right of first refusal to extend or renew this Agreement beyond the stated Term.
