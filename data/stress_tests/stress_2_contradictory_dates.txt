THIS LEASE AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made and entered into this eleventh day of February, two thousand twenty-six, by and between the Landlord and Northbrook Family Dentistry (hereinafter referred to as "Tenant"), regarding the real property located at 305 Willowbrook Ave, Suite 110 (the "Premises").

Section 2.1 (Term). The term of this lease shall commence on March 1, 2026, and shall continue for a period of sixty full calendar months, expiring on February 28, 2031.

In consideration for the rights of use and occupancy granted under this Agreement, Tenant covenants to pay to the Landlord a monthly base rent in the amount of Four Thousand Four Hundred and 00/100 Dollars, payable in advance on or before the first business day of each calendar month. Commencing on the first anniversary of the Commencement Date, and on each successive anniversary thereafter, the monthly base rent shall be subject to a fixed escalation adjustment of three percent of the then-current monthly base rent.

Tenant shall reimburse Landlord for its proportional share of Common Area Maintenance, fixed at ten percent of total aggregate costs, payable within fifteen days of receipt of an invoice.

Section 9.4 (Term Confirmation). For the avoidance of doubt, the parties reconfirm that the Term of this Agreement shall expire on February 28, 2030, and Landlord shall have no obligation to provide notice of such expiration.

Provided Tenant is not in default, Tenant shall be granted one option to extend this occupancy for an additional period of five years, exercisable by written notice given not less than one hundred fifty days prior to the expiration of the Term.
