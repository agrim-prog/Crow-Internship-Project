THIS LEASE AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made and entered into this fifth day of January, two thousand twenty-five, by and between the Landlord and Palmetto Fitness Group LLC (hereinafter referred to as the "Original Tenant"), regarding the real property located at 812 Ridgeline Dr, Suite 2 (the "Premises").

The term of this lease shall commence on February 1, 2025, and shall continue for a period of seventy-two full calendar months, expiring on January 31, 2031.

In consideration for the rights of use and occupancy granted under this Agreement, Original Tenant covenants to pay to the Landlord a monthly base rent in the amount of Five Thousand One Hundred and 00/100 Dollars, payable in advance on or before the first business day of each calendar month. Commencing on the first anniversary of the Commencement Date, and on each successive anniversary thereafter, the monthly base rent shall be subject to a fixed escalation adjustment of four percent of the then-current monthly base rent.

Original Tenant shall reimburse Landlord for its proportional share of Common Area Maintenance, fixed at twelve percent of total aggregate costs, payable within fifteen days of receipt of an invoice.

ASSIGNMENT ADDENDUM (effective June 1, 2027): With Landlord's written consent, Original Tenant has assigned all of its right, title, and interest in this Agreement to Summit Wellness Partners Inc., which assignee assumes and shall hereafter be bound by all obligations of the Tenant under this Agreement for the remainder of the Term, on the same rent, escalation, and CAM terms set forth above. Original Tenant is hereby released from further liability under this Agreement as of the effective date of assignment.

Provided the then-current tenant is not in default, such tenant shall be granted one option to extend this occupancy for an additional period of three years, exercisable by written notice given not less than one hundred twenty days prior to the expiration of the Term.
