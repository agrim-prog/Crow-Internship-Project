THIS LEASE AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made and entered into this twenty-fifth day of June, two thousand twenty-six, by and between the Landlord and Silverline Dental Group (hereinafter referred to as "Tenant"), regarding the real property located at 88 Molarview Terrace (the "Premises").
The term of this lease shall commence on September 1, 2026, and shall continue for a period of sixty full calendar months, expiring on August 31, 2031. In consideration for the rights of use and occupancy granted under this Agreement, Tenant covenants to pay to the Landlord a monthly base rent in the amount of Five Thousand Two Hundred and 00/100 Dollars.
Beginning on the second anniversary of the Commencement Date, the monthly base rent shall increase by Three Hundred and 00/100 Dollars ($300.00) per month, and shall increase by an additional Three Hundred and 00/100 Dollars ($300.00) per month on each anniversary thereafter for the remainder of the term.
Tenant shall contribute toward shared building costs in a manner and amount to be reasonably determined by the Landlord from time to time, consistent with practices at comparable properties in the vicinity.
Provided the Tenant is not in default, the Tenant shall be granted a single option to extend this occupancy for one additional period of five years, exercisable by written notice given at a commercially reasonable time prior to the expiration of the initial term.
