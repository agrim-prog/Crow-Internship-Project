THIS LEASE AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made and entered into this third day of February, two thousand twenty-seven, by and between the Landlord and Bolt Fitness Studio (hereinafter referred to as "Tenant"), regarding the real property located at 300 Ironworks Ln (the "Premises").

In consideration for the rights of use and occupancy granted under this Agreement, Tenant covenants to pay to the Landlord a monthly base rent in the amount of Two Thousand Two Hundred and 00/100 Dollars, payable in advance on the first day of each calendar month.
