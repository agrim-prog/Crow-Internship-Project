THIS LEASE AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made and entered into this ninth day of January, two thousand twenty-seven, by and between the Landlord and Velvet Bakery Co. (hereinafter referred to as "Tenant"), regarding the real property located at 22 Flourmill Row (the "Premises").
The term of this lease shall commence on 01/09/2027, and shall continue for a period of twenty-four full calendar months, expiring on December 31, 2028. In consideration for the rights of use and occupancy granted under this Agreement, Tenant covenants to pay to the Landlord a monthly base rent in the amount of  One  Thousand   Nine Hundred  and 00/100  Dollars  (extra spacing as filed). Said payments shall be remitted in advance on or before the first business day of each calendar month.
No escalation clause is included in this instrument; the base rent shall remain fixed for the entirety of the term.
Tenant's obligations with respect to shared building costs are addressed in a separate operating agreement not attached hereto.
The Tenant shall have no right to extend or renew this Agreement beyond the stated term absent a new written agreement executed by both parties.
